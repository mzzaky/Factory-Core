How the Supply Chain Works

Players mine vanilla ores (Iron Ore, Coal, Diamond Ore, etc.) from the Minecraft world and deposit them into a Smeltery. The Smeltery converts vanilla ores into plugin-tracked Raw Resources (Tier I). These raw resources then feed into Tier II factories (Steel Mill, Workshop, Refinery) to create Advanced Resources, Machine Parts, and Fuel. Finally, Tier III factories (Military Factory, Electronics Assembly, Automotive Plant, Aerospace Foundry) combine Tier II materials into high-value Final Products that can be sold directly or fulfilled through Company Events for bonus payouts.

Events

Company Demand Events

Virtual companies periodically request products from players. Fulfill their orders within the time limit for guaranteed payouts with bonus multipliers.

📜

How Events Work

Each event has a chance to appear (higher % = more common), a time limit in seconds, and a demand list of resources to deliver. The price offer is the base payout (typically 18-22% above resource value). A bonus multiplier is applied if you complete the order before the bonus condition (% of time remaining). For example, a 1.3× bonus at 50% means you earn 30% extra if you finish in the first half of the deadline.

Always Aim for the Early Bonus

Every event includes a bonus multiplier (1.2×–1.5×) that activates if you deliver before a certain deadline percentage. For the biggest events, this can mean an extra $3,000–$5,000. Keep a stockpile of high-demand products ready so you can fulfill orders immediately when they appear. The best strategy is to pre-produce and stockpile Tier III products rather than starting production after an event appears.

Balance Frequency vs. Payout

Strategy

Economy Tips & Strategies

Proven strategies to maximize your income and grow your industrial empire efficiently.

⛏️

Use Fortune Pickaxes for Raw Material Batches

Fortune III pickaxes drop Raw Iron, Raw Copper, and Raw Gold — which unlock the batch smelting recipes. These batch recipes are far more efficient than ore-block recipes. A single Raw Iron Batch produces 20 Industrial Iron Ore for just $25, compared to 4 units from a standard ore recipe. Always bring a Fortune pickaxe to mining trips.

🏗️

Build Your Factories in the Right Order

Start with a Smeltery (required for everything). Next, build a Workshop and Steel Mill simultaneously — they both consume raw resources and feed Tier III. Add a Refinery only when you have a Tier III factory that needs fuel. For your first Tier III factory, choose Electronics Assembly (Solar Panel Array has 48% margin) or Aerospace Foundry (Satellite Module has 51% margin) depending on your budget.

📦

Stockpile Tier II Materials Before Going Tier III

Tier III recipes require many different Tier II inputs. Before starting production on products like Jet Engines or Satellite Modules, build up a stockpile of at least 10–20 units of each required Advanced Resource, Machine Part, and Fuel type. Running out of one component mid-production wastes time and opportunity cost.

🏢

Target Multi-Product Company Events

Mixed/cross-tier events like Forward Base Supply Drop ($10,540+) and Launch Vehicle Assembly ($10,560+) pay enormously but require diverse production capabilities. Having multiple Tier III factories running simultaneously lets you fulfill these mega-orders. The bonus alone on a Launch Vehicle Assembly can exceed $5,000.

💹

Prioritize High Margin, Not Just High Price

A Rocket Booster sells for $3,500 (43% margin, +$1,515 profit) while a Satellite Module sells for $3,000 but has a 51% margin (+$1,525 profit). The Satellite Module actually earns more profit with lower input costs and less production time. Always check the margin column in the recipe tables — percentage margin tells you more about efficiency than raw sell price.

⏱️

Consider Profit per Minute, Not Just per Cycle

A Smartphone ($191 profit in 8.3 minutes = $23/min) may seem worse than a Satellite Module ($1,525 profit in 20 minutes = $76/min). But if you can run 3 Smartphone cycles in the time of one Satellite cycle, throughput matters. For factories you check frequently, shorter recipes can outperform long ones. For AFK production, go with the highest single-cycle profit.

🌐

Diversify Across Event Companies

Don't focus exclusively on one company's events. Stellar Aerospace events pay the most but appear rarely (0.8–2% chance). Meanwhile, companies like Cobalt Mining, Pinnacle MFG, and NexGen Electronics post frequent, easy-to-fill orders. A steady income from common events plus occasional mega-payouts from rare events is the path to consistent wealth.

🔄

Vertical Integration is King

The most profitable players control the entire supply chain. If you own a Smeltery, Steel Mill, Workshop, Refinery, and an Aerospace Foundry, every Satellite Module you produce costs only the mining time + production fees ($200 fee) — you don't pay market prices for intermediate materials. This makes your effective margin significantly higher than the 51% shown in tables.
