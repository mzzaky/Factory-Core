1. Creating a Factory

The first step to building your industrial empire is establishing a base of operations. In FactoryCore, you need to register a Region in the world where your factory will operate.

Step-by-Step Procedure:

  1. Obtain the selection wand: Use the command //wand to get the WorldEdit selection tool (usually a wooden axe).
  2. Select the area: Left-click one corner of your desired factory area, and right-click the opposite corner to select the region boundaries.
  3. Define the region: Use the WorldGuard command /rg define <region_id> to officially protect and register the selected area.
  4. Execute the creation command: Link the region to FactoryCore using the admin command:
    /fc admin create <region_id> <factory_id> <factory_type> <price_value>.
  5. Confirmation: Upon success, your region is officially recognized as a Factory by the plugin. You can now access its main management interface!
💡 Developer Tip
Make sure you have enough in-game balance if the server has a configured economy cost for registering new factories!

2. Hiring Employees

A factory cannot run on its own. You need workers to operate the machinery and process resources. Adding employees to your factory brings it to life and boosts the production rate.

How to Register and Assign Employees:

  1. Open the Factory GUI: Use /factory gui or interact with the factory block to open the main menu.
  2. Navigate to Employees Center: Click on the "Employees" menu icon to see your workforce overview.
  3. Hire an Employee: Purchase an employee contract from the available NPC list. Each employee type has different production bonuses and salary requirements.
  4. Spawn / Register the NPC: Once hired, place the employee inside your factory region. They will become an active NPC worker representing your workforce!
⚖️ Salary Payments
Remember that active employees generate salary invoices over time. If you fail to pay their salaries through the Invoice Center, they may stop working!

3. Producing Items

Once your factory is established and your employees are working, it's time to manufacture goods. Production is the heart of your economy, turning raw materials into highly profitable products.

Understanding the Production Mechanism:

  1. Provide Input Resources: Factories require specific input materials. Deposit the required items into the factory's Input Storage.
  2. Wait for the Cycle: The production cycle runs automatically on a scheduled timer. The speed depends on the factory type, installed research, and employee buffs.
  3. Harvesting the Output: Once a production cycle completes, the newly synthesized goods are moved to the Output Storage.
  4. Claim Your Items: Left-click or interact with your factory NPC (or Output Storage blocks) to open the output inventory and claim your newly manufactured items!
💰 Next Steps
Take the items you just harvested and sell them in the Marketplace for a massive profit, or use them as input for higher-tier factories!
