Overview

FactoryCore fully supports Executable Items (EI) out of the box! This tight coupling lets you seamlessly employ EI's highly interactive, scripted, and trigger-based items directly within your factories, marketplaces, and production chains.

💡 Endless Possibilities
By utilizing Executable Items, you can craft powerful factory tools that activate special server commands when right-clicked, or create highly valuable consumable items that grand special buffs, and have your factories produce them natively!

How to Setup

Like other plugin hooks, mapping an Executable Item to FactoryCore is effortless. The integration automatically boots up alongside FactoryCore. You define your Executable Items directly in the resources.yml file using just one crucial attribute:

  • executable-items-id: The exact identifier/ID of the item configured within the ExecutableItems plugin (e.g., MAGIC_DRILL).

After being linked, the item behaves exactly like a regular FactoryCore resource, allowing players to buy, sell, craft, or demand it as a factory input!

Configuration Examples

Here is how you securely map an Executable Item to a native FactoryCore resource in your resources.yml:

YAML 
resources:
  ei_magic_drill:
    # The FactoryCore internal category (e.g. RAW_RESOURCES, PROCESSED_GOODS)
    type: "PROCESSED_GOODS"
    
    # The fallback material if ExecutableItems is uninstalled or disables
    material: "DIAMOND_PICKAXE"
    name: "&bMagic Drill"
    
    # === EXECUTABLE ITEMS HOOK ===
    executable-items-id: "MAGIC_DRILL"
    
    # Marketplace suggested price config (FactoryCore logic)
    suggested_price: 12500.0
⚖️ Attribute Overrides
When executable-items-id is provided natively, FactoryCore lets ExecutableItems entirely handle the item building. Standard FactoryCore visual elements like material, name, lore, and custom-model-data will be overridden by the ExecutableItems metadata, yet internal attributes such as suggested_price are securely preserved.

Tips & Tricks

🛠️ Crafting Active Tools
Define an Executable Item as a factory's recipe result! For instance, players could deposit hundreds of raw materials into a highly advanced factory to slowly print a powerful scripted "Plasma Cannon" or "Speed Booster" logic item!
  • If the server owner completely uninstalls ExecutableItems, FactoryCore's robust fallback architecture activates. Your custom resource smoothly converts back into a vanilla representation (using the defined material and name variables), stopping major economical bugs.
  • FactoryCore flawlessly communicates with the ExecutableItems API to perfectly serialize and deserialize data. Even if your players modify their item durability or stats, FactoryCore recognizes exactly which ID it originated from!
  • Double-check that the executable-items-id matches exactly what you configured in ExecutableItems (spelling & casing matter).
