Overview

The Daily Quests system is an engagement tool designed to offer players routine goals, rewarding them for their operational consistency. Quests automatically hook into core functions like production cycles, paying taxes, or marketplace trading, granting experience and currency rewards to speed up their empire's growth.

💡 Why Daily Quests?
Daily quests keep your economy active and incentivize regular logins, pushing players to consistently manage their factories and trade resources, ensuring that the marketplace and tax ecosystems stay vibrant.

Mechanics & Refresh

Quests operate on a strict 24-hour cycle. Progression is seamlessly and silently tracked in the background via event listeners seamlessly woven into the plugin's ecosystem.

  • Global Reset: At the configured reset-hour (based on your server's timezone), all player progresses are completely wiped and quests are refreshed for a brand new cycle.
  • Automatic Sync: The system automatically manages offline player data. If a player is offline during the reset phase, their stats reset immediately when they log back in.
  • Toast Notifications: Completing a quest perfectly mimics vanilla behavior with a toast notification popup and distinctive sound effect.
  • Completion Bonus: A specialized bonus reward is assigned when a player has painstakingly completed every single daily quest within the 24-hour window.

Quest Types

The system natively checks against these core plugin constants:

🛠️

PRODUCTION_COMPLETE

Tracked whenever a factory output securely transfers a manufactured recipe output item to its storage.

💸

SALARY_PAYMENT

Incremented every single time a player actively pays off an employee salary fee directly from the GUI.

📜

TAX_PAYMENT

Incremented each time a scheduled factory maintenance property tax is paid to the state.

📈

MARKETPLACE_SELL

Counts successful item transactions where the player directly listed and successfully sold items off the global market.

🛒

MARKETPLACE_BUY

Counts exactly how many times a player purchased essential resource quantities using the global market interface.

Configuration Reference

All quest attributes are structured cleanly inside the daily-quest.yml file. Define names, descriptions, icons, targets, and economic balancing right here.

YAML 
settings:
  enabled: true
  # Hour of the day (0-23) when daily quests reset (server timezone)
  reset-hour: 0

quests:
  industrial_hustle:
    name: "&6Industrial Hustle"
    description: "Complete 12 daily productions"
    icon: FURNACE
    target: 12
    type: PRODUCTION_COMPLETE
    reward:
      exp: 100
      money: 5000.0

# Bonus reward when ALL daily quests are completed
all-complete-bonus:
  enabled: true
  exp: 500
  money: 25000.0
⚖️ Economy Balancing Note
Ensure your completion numbers (target) are realistically achievable for an average player within 24 hours while restricting the money reward to limit inflation loops.

Tips & Tricks

📊 Visual Progress Context
You can perfectly complement the Daily Quests plugin by configuring Research Buffs! For example, assigning a "Task Productivity Booster" via the Research feature can multiply these specific standard standard task rewards.
  • Players can check their ongoing quest completion status from the main control menus. As an admin, make sure the configured icon explicitly aligns with the targeted theme.
  • If testing via your localhost development environment, temporarily adjust reset-hour to verify data resets.
